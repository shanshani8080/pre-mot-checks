Pre-MOT Checks You Can Do on Your Own Driveway

Plenty of MOT failures come down to things an owner could have spotted in ten minutes. Before you book, run a mot history check on the car so you can see what was flagged last year, then walk round it with a friend and work through the list below.

Outside the car

  • Switch on every light, including the rear fog lamp and the number plate lamp, and have someone confirm each one works.
  • Measure tyre tread with a 20p coin. If the outer band of the coin is visible, the tread may be near the 1.6mm legal limit.
  • Look for bulges or cuts in the tyre sidewalls.
  • Check the number plates are clean, readable and not cracked.

Inside the car

  • Pull every seatbelt hard and make sure it locks and retracts.
  • Press the horn. It needs to give a steady, single tone.
  • Start the engine and confirm that no warning lights stay on, especially the engine management, airbag and ABS lights.
  • Top up screenwash and try the wipers. Torn blades leave streaks and can count against you.

Under the bonnet

Brake fluid and oil levels are worth a quick look. The tester will not top them up for you, and a car with very low oil may be refused an emissions test.
